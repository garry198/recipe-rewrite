All measurements and oven temperatures in the source have already been converted for the German market and replaced by placeholders such as [[Q1]] (a complete measurement WITH its unit, e.g. "225 g", "240 ml", "2 EL", "20 x 10 cm") or [[T1]] (a complete temperature, e.g. "180 °C Ober-/Unterhitze (160 °C Umluft)").
